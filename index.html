Frequently Asked Questions

What areas does MetarPath cover?
MetarPath uses the FAA Aviation Weather Center API, so weather data is available for airports in the United States and Canada.
Why don't I see any stations for my route?
Not every airport has an automated weather reporting station (AWOS/ASOS). If a station along your route doesn't report METAR/TAF data, it won't appear in results.
Is MetarPath a certified flight planning tool?
No. MetarPath is for informational purposes only and is not a substitute for an official weather briefing. Always obtain a complete, official briefing before any flight.
